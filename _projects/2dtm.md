We develop statistical theory and software for **2D template matching (2DTM)** — locating known biomolecular structures inside crowded, low-SNR cryo-EM images. Recent work introduces a new statistical metric that improves detection sensitivity for small targets (sub-50 kDa complexes) and quantifies false-positive rates.
