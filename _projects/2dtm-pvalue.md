Detecting smaller targets by 2D template matching (2DTM) is difficult because the detected signal depends on the molecular weight of the target. Low-resolution contrast can be a reliable indicator of a target, but is down-weighted in the standard 2DTM workflow. We address these challenges by developing a new statistical metric — the **2DTM p-value** — that improves the detection of several previously challenging targets, including a 193 kDa clathrin monomer.

The 2DTM p-value is particularly useful for detecting targets of 50 kDa and smaller, and for reliably detecting rare targets that require higher detection thresholds to suppress false positives.

**Publication:** [A new statistical metric for robust target detection in cryo-EM using 2D template matching](https://doi.org/10.1107/S2052252524011771) · *IUCrJ* (2025).
