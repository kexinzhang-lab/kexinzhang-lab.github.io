Samuel, Class of 2029, is from Tampa, Florida and is majoring in Molecular Biophysics and Biochemistry while pursuing a certificate in Data Science. His research interests center on how mitochondrial stress and ROS alter proteasome function, protein quality control, and stress adaptation in _S. cerevisiae_. Additionally, he is interested in using cryo-EM/2DTM to study proteasome abundance, localization, and conformational state shift across cellular environments.
