**Kexin Zhang** is the Principal Investigator of the Computational Imaging and Discovery Lab. Her research develops computational methods at the intersection of cryo-electron microscopy, machine learning, and structural biology, with a focus on differentiable pose inference, 2D template matching, and direct atomic-model refinement from cryo-EM images.

She holds a PhD from the University of Michigan, where she worked on multiscale modeling of RNA structures using NMR chemical shifts. Prior to launching the lab, she was a postdoctoral researcher developing methods for sub-50 kDa cryo-EM reconstruction and statistical metrics for robust target detection.
