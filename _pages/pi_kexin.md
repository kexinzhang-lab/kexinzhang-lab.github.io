**Kexin Zhang** is the Principal Investigator of the Computational Imaging and Discovery Lab. She will join the [Department of Molecular Biophysics and Biochemistry](https://mbb.yale.edu) at Yale University as an Assistant Professor in **Fall 2026**. Her research develops computational methods at the intersection of cryo-electron microscopy, machine learning, and structural biology, with a focus on differentiable pose inference, 2D template matching, and direct atomic-model refinement from cryo-EM images.

Kexin holds a PhD in Chemistry and Scientific Computing from the University of Michigan, where she worked on RNA structure prediction by integrating experimental NMR data with machine learning. Before launching the lab, she was a postdoctoral researcher in Niko Grigorieff's lab at UMass Chan Medical School, where she developed computational methods to push the cryo-EM detection limit toward sub-50 kDa complexes.
