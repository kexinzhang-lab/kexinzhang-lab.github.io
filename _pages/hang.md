Hang Li is a BBS student in the BQBS track. He received his B.A. in Biology and Mathematics from Grinnell College and his M.S. in Biostatistics from Yale University. His research interests broadly lie at the intersection of cancer biology, chromosome instability, quantitative imaging, and computational biology. In his free time, he enjoys rock climbing, riding motorcycles, and going to the beach.
